# The Endurance

`endurance-lod2.bin`, `endurance-lod1.bin`, `endurance.bin` and `endurance-full.bin` are derived from **"Interstellar | Endurance"** by **devPilot**
(@devPilot0615), <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/interstellar-endurance-901fec2809704b74bec891e9a40a8726>,
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: generated by `bun scripts/build-endurance.ts` from the model's FBX (Blender:
`scripts/endurance-convert.py`) — its objects joined, triangulated and decimated into levels of detail —
`endurance-lod2.bin` 5 % of the triangles (12 k), `endurance-lod1.bin` 10 % (24 k), `endurance.bin` 40 %
(96 k), `endurance-full.bin` all of them (240 k); centred on the ring and scaled to a
diameter of 1, the ring's axis on z; normals smoothed (40°), a material id per part and ambient
occlusion baked per vertex; the model's textures are not used (the app shades it per part).
`src/endurance.ts` (the ship on its orbit round Gargantua) picks a level by the ship's size on screen,
each downloaded when first needed; `src/ship.ts` (the Endurance flown, docked) uses `endurance.bin`.
One level alone: `bun scripts/build-endurance.ts [src] [blender] endurance-lod1,…`.
These derived files are distributed under the same licence.

Format (little endian): `"ENDR"`, `u32` version 1, vertex count, index count, 6 × `f32` bounds (min, max),
then vertices (8 × `f32`: position, normal, material, ambient occlusion) and `u32` indices.
