# The F-35B

`f35.bin` and the six maps (`f35-{albedo,normal,mr}-{0,1}.ktx2`) are derived from **"F-35A Lightning II"** by
**shangus930**, <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/f-35a-lightning-ii-a06d6113cfb44a0aa7b8f17106aca9c4>, licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (supplied as `assets/airplanes/F-35B Lightning II.glb`, not in
the repository).

Changes: generated by `scripts/build-f35.py` (Blender) and `scripts/build-f35-maps.py` — turned into the ship's frame
(x to its left, y up, z its nose), scaled to the real craft (×0.535: 10.7 m span, 15.4 m long), the belly at y = 0;
the gear legs, the helmet and the nozzle decimated; the control surfaces cut out of the airframe along their hinge
lines (the flaperons, the rudders) or separated (the stabilators, the leading-edge flaps); the gear's clip sampled into
tracks; the wheels, the nozzle's petals, the canopy and the pilot made parts of their own; ambient occlusion baked per
vertex; the ten materials' maps gathered into two layers of atlases and re-encoded as KTX2 (UASTC). These derived files
are distributed under the same licence.

Format (little endian, gzip): see the header of `scripts/build-f35.py` ("F35B" v1: bounds, the part table, the tracks,
then vertices of 10 × `f32` — position, normal, material, part + ambient occlusion, UV — and `u32` indices).
