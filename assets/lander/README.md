# The Lander

`lander.bin` and the three maps (`lander-albedo.webp`, `lander-normal.webp`, `lander-lights.webp`) are
derived from **"Endurance Lander from Interstellar"** by **Crusty Bread** (@CrustyBread),
<https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/endurance-lander-from-interstellar-0bcbd25523794779826e3e19770dc1f2>,
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: generated by `bun scripts/build-lander.ts` from the model's OBJ and textures — n-gons
triangulated; turned into the ship's frame (x to its left, y up, z its nose), scaled to 24 m long, the
belly at y = 0; ambient occlusion baked per vertex; the colour, normal and lights maps re-encoded as WebP
(2048²). These derived files are distributed under the same licence.

Format (little endian): `"LNDR"`, `u32` version 1, vertex count, index count, 6 × `f32` bounds (min, max),
then vertices (10 × `f32`: position, normal, material, ambient occlusion, UV) and `u32` indices.
