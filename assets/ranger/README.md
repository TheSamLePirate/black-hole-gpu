# The Ranger

`ranger.bin` is derived from **"Interstellar Ranger One"** by **Max Vizell** (@explay.xx),
<https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/interstellar-ranger-one-77c63df2062d4fd9863cc64711450c6f>,
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: generated by `bun scripts/build-ranger.ts` from the model's OBJ — n-gons triangulated, the mesh
refined (crack-free) to ≤ 0.45 m edges, normals smoothed (40° auto smooth) on the coarse mesh and
interpolated into the refined triangles, a material id per part and ambient occlusion baked per vertex;
the model's textures are not used (the app shades the hull procedurally).
This derived file is distributed under the same licence.

Format (little endian): `"RNGR"`, `u32` version 2, vertex count, index count, 6 × `f32` bounds (min, max),
then vertices (8 × `f32`: position, normal, material, ambient occlusion) and `u32` indices.

# The cockpit

`cockpit.bin` is derived from **"Interstellar Ranger One Cockpit"** (Sketchfab), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) — supplied as an OBJ (objects "Ra…": the floor,
the consoles, the seats, the cryo pods, the screens, the glass), without its maps.

Changes: `scripts/cockpit-convert.py` (Blender: each object's kind from its name, the two flight sticks
found, each object decimated to a share of a 150 k-triangle budget — the screens and the glass kept whole —,
normals smoothed below 35°, each loose part's size and a hash), then `bun scripts/build-cockpit.ts` (placed
in the Ranger's frame at its own scale, its windows on the hull's; ambient occlusion and the sky seen
through its windows baked per vertex; the screens' UVs made 0…1 each, each screen given its display by
where it is — before the pilot the attitude and the target, before the copilot the orbit and the systems,
between the seats the docking and the plan, the clocks and the log beside the seats and aft; the sticks'
pivots): 76 k triangles, 3.1 MB.

    /Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup \
        --python scripts/cockpit-convert.py -- "assets/Interstellar Ranger One Cockpit/endurance lander cockpit.obj" /tmp/cockpit-raw.bin
    bun scripts/build-cockpit.ts [/tmp/cockpit-raw.bin]

The app (`src/ship.ts`, its own lean shader) shades it procedurally — the materials, the lights, the
screens' pictures (the flight's telemetry drawn by `src/ui/cockpitscreens.ts`). This derived file is
distributed under the same licence.

Format (little endian, gzip): `"CKPT"`, `u32` version 2, vertex count, index count, 6 × `f32` bounds, `u32`
stick count and per stick 4 × `f32` (its pivot, 0), then vertices (10 × `f32`: position, normal, material,
ambient occlusion, then for the screens their display and u, v; for the rest the sky seen and the part's
size [cm] + its hash) and `u32` indices. Materials 60–72: floor, walls, consoles, seats, cryo pods, bags,
metal, laptops, screens, TARS's platform, airlock, glass, the sticks' grips.
