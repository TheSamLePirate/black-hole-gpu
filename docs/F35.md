# The F-35B Lightning II

*[Version française](F35.fr.md)*

A fighter flown over the real Earth: the F-35B with its STOVL, its cockpit, its helmet, flight plans,
challenges and fights. The plan and its rounds: [PLAN-F35.md](PLAN-F35.md); the progress sheets:
`docs/progress/f35/`.

## Getting in

- **Fly the F-35** (pause menu) or **Prepare a flight** (the radial wheel, Tab held, while flying a plane):
  an airfield, a start (lined up on the runway in use, on a 15 km final, cruising 40 km out), the
  airfield's own hour, the weather, the fuel, a flight plan to another airfield — **Fly**.
- **Challenges and fights** (the radial wheel, the pause menu): gate courses, graded landings, aerobatics,
  and four fights.
- Choosing the F-35 anywhere else puts it on the nearest runway; a craft left on a runway stays there
  (parked: the next one lines up clear of it).

## Keys (QWERTY names — the physical keys: AZERTY's in brackets)

| Keys | What |
|---|---|
| W S · A D · Q E (Z S · Q D · A E) | Pitch · roll · yaw |
| Shift · Alt (or ↑ ↓) | Throttle up · down (held) |
| Z · X (W · X) | Full throttle · cut |
| ⇧Z (⇧W) | Afterburner (the throttle in its detent) |
| ' · ⇧' (ù · ⇧ù) | Autopilot: altitude and heading held (the stick flies through them) · autothrottle |
| ⇧M | NAV: the autopilot flies the flight plan (LNAV and VNAV); the stick takes it off |
| ⇧O | The flight plan's panel |
| ⇧B | STOVL mode: the lift fan, the swivelled nozzle; below 65 m/s the hover law |
| G · P · ⇧P | Gear · flaps · air brake |
| U | Automatic take-off |
| Space (held) · Enter · Tab · = · − | In a fight: the gun · the weapon chosen · the radar's lock · the next weapon · flares and chaff (the pause on Esc then) |
| V · ⇧T | The next view · a tripod on the ground |
| T | SAS |

A HOTAS works too ([HOTAS.md](HOTAS.md)): its axes, the throttle lever with its afterburner detent.

## The aircraft

- **Model**: "F-35A Lightning II" by shangus930 (Sketchfab, CC-BY-4.0), rebuilt by `scripts/build-f35.py`
  (Blender): control surfaces carved and hinged, the gear and its doors from the model's own clip, the
  canopy, the lift fan and its doors, the three-bearing swivel nozzle, the cockpit's stick, throttle and
  panoramic display; KTX2 maps (`scripts/build-f35-maps.py`).
- **Flight** (`src/f35/engine.ts`, `src/vessels.ts`, `src/pilot.ts`): the F135 (120 kN dry, 176 kN wet,
  its lapse with height and Mach, its spool, its fuel — 6 125 kg inside); a fly-by-wire law (roll 165°/s,
  7 g, the angle of attack limited); wave drag. Measured (`tests/f35-*.test.ts`): take-off in 10.5 s and
  358 m at 144 kt; M 1.60 at 11 km with the afterburner; stall 137 kt; 37 min at military power, 10 min
  wet.
- **STOVL** (`src/f35/stovl.ts`, `src/controller/stovl.ts`): ⇧B converts in 6 s (the doors, the clutch);
  the nozzle comes down as it slows; below 65 m/s the hover law: the stick a speed over the ground, the
  throttle keys a vertical speed, let go — held over its point.
- **Cockpit** (`src/f35/pcd.ts`, `src/f35/cockpit.ts`): the panoramic touch display drawn live — TSD (the
  moving map with the route), FCS, ENG, FUEL (the bingo), STOVL, CNI, ADI, checklists —, touched with the
  mouse; the helmet's symbology (no HUD glass), its NAV annunciators and the active waypoint.
- **Sound** (`src/audio/engine.ts`): the turbofan's whine and roar, the afterburner's crackle, the lift
  fan, dulled from the seat.
- **Effects** (F7): the vapour sheet torn off the wing pulling hard, the wingtips' threads, the heat haze
  behind the nozzle (setting *Heat haze*), the grey-out and the red-out with a felt load that lags, the
  view's shake (setting *Flight shake*).

## Flight plans (F8)

`src/game/flightplan.ts`, `src/controller/fplan.ts`, `src/ui/routepanel.ts`.

- **⇧O**: the Earth's map, zoomable; a click adds a waypoint (on an airfield: the airfield), after the one
  selected; each point's altitude and speed constraints, its leg's course and length; reverse, direct-to.
- **Fuel**: each leg's burn at its level and speed (the simulator's own consumption), what is left on
  arrival, the bingo now — its alert when crossed.
- **NAV (⇧M)**: LNAV flies the legs along their great circles (the turns anticipated, a direct-to when far
  off the route), VNAV the climb to the level and the descent on a 3° path from its top.
- Shown on the TSD (the route, the active point, the cross-track), in the helmet (VNAV · LNAV, the waypoint
  and its caret on the heading tape); kept in the saves.

## Challenges (F9)

`src/game/challenges.ts`, `src/controller/challenge.ts`, `src/ui/challengepanel.ts`.

- **Gate courses**: the Welsh Mach Loop (11 gates under 250 m), Rainbow Canyon (5), the Rogers slalom (8):
  a gate missed costs 10 s (said which way: left, right, high, low), each second over the ceiling one.
- **Graded landing**: the sink, the aim point (300 m), the centreline, the reference speed (140 kt).
- **Vertical landing**: the distance from the pad, the sink, the drift.
- **Aerobatics**: a roll, a loop, a roll — heading and height held, the loop round and in its plane.
- Medals against each par; the bests kept.

## Fights (F10)

`src/game/combat.ts`, `src/game/scenarios.ts`, `src/controller/combat.ts`.

- **Weapons**: the 25 mm gun (220 rounds), the AIM-120C (radar, beyond 15 km) and the AIM-9X (infrared,
  off the nose) flown by proportional navigation, the GBU-32 JDAM glided to its point, flares and chaff.
- **Radar**: the targets before the nose within a range for their size; Tab locks the next. Its scope on the
  helmet (bottom left, heading up): each contact's chevron with its track and its height against ours, the
  ground targets, the missiles in flight (theirs red, ours cyan) with their tracks, the lock ringed and named,
  the range rings (set, or chosen to hold the lock and the nearest threats); in a challenge the next gates.
- **Threats**: aggressor fighters (patrol, engage with their missile and gun, defend with decoys), an SA-11
  site that fires at whatever flies over 150 m within its reach.
- **The HUD**: target boxes, the lock with its range and closure, the gun's pipper on the lead (red when the
  shot is on), the launch zone and SHOOT in red (the lock's box red, the steering dot in the ASE circle) — IN
  RANGE · TURN in amber off the nose —, the bomb's fall point, the missile warning and its bearings, the
  damage. The weapons' panel (right): the gun's rounds as a bar, each missile and bomb a box, the one chosen
  framed, empty ones red, ∞ with infinite ammunition.
- **Fights**: drone hunt, guns only, two-ship intercept, ground strike — the Mojave's ranges.
- The aggressors are drawn as F-35s (the only fighter mesh), their missiles' smoke as trails, the
  explosions over the view.

## Avionics and settings

- **Avionics** (helmet, top left): the engine's spool, the throttle and the thrust, the afterburner, the fuel
  with its bingo mark, the burn and the time left, the gear, flaps and air brake, the autopilot's modes (ALT
  HDG A/T LNAV VNAV), the load against its limit, STOVL, the damage and the three first cautions.
- Flown, the F-35 leaves out a spacecraft's instruments: no attitude ball, no orbit, no target among the
  planets, no orbital markers; Tab taps lock the radar in a fight (never step through the bodies), Tab held
  opens the jet's own wheel (autopilot, afterburner, STOVL, gear, weapons, flight plan, challenges); the
  orbital holds, the roll alignment and the orbital autopilots are refused; F keeps its own flight laws.
- **Settings** (group *F-35*): G effects, vapour, flight shake, heat haze, the G limit (5–9 g), infinite fuel,
  infinite ammunition, invulnerable, the aggressors' skill (easy, normal, ace), enemy missiles, the radar
  scope and its range, the weapons' panel, the avionics, the helmet's colour (green, amber, cyan, white) and
  brightness.

## Setting down

Placed on a runway, the craft stands on the drawn runway's axis (WGS84), its nose down the end in use, level
on its gear — whatever the view (`tests/e2e/runway-placement.e2e.test.ts`). Set down anywhere else, it sits
on the plane through the ground under each of its legs: tilted with a hillside, every wheel on the ground
(`tests/e2e/ground-placement.e2e.test.ts`).

## Performance

Measured on an M1 Max at 1600 × 1000, the same place (Edwards), `__bh.game.perf({ gpu: true })` — Apple's
pass timestamps overlap, read them as orders of magnitude:

| View | GPU frame | Ship pass (shading) |
|---|---|---|
| Ranger, chase | 11–17 ms | 9–13 ms |
| F-35, chase | 11–16 ms | 9–14 ms |
| F-35, cockpit | 9–12 ms | 7–9 ms |
| F-35, afterburner (the plume) | 10 ms | + 0.7–1 ms |
| F-35, guns duel (an aggressor 300 m ahead) | 5–7 ms | 6 ms |

60 frames a second held in each.
