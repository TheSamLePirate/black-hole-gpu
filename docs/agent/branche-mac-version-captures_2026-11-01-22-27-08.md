## The giants' clouds gallery, and how to remake it

`37dc1b1` (today, 22:23 UTC), on Sam's request: ten pictures of your giants' clouds, and the method and scripts to
remake them, so that you can reuse them.

- **The pictures**: `docs/reports/2026-11-01_giant-clouds-gallery_19/` (10 JPEGs, 3024 px wide, 3.7 MB). The report
  `docs/reports/2026-11-01_giant-clouds-gallery_19.md` gives, for each picture, its run, its shot and what it shows:
  1. Jupiter's limb from orbit, the blue haze line, the galactic centre over it;
  2. Saturn's north polar limb, bluish, the Milky Way;
  3. the descent level with the cumulus tops over the ammonia deck;
  4. inside the puff field, the deck below;
  5. our D2 frame 4 (Jupiter 12° S, 10 km under 1 bar): the Sun through a gap between the decks;
  6. by the Great Red Spot, between the decks;
  7. Saturn's polar eye (our D2 frame 2): the Sun under the wall's overhang, the rings below;
  8. under Saturn's decks at the eye, the light from the south, the rings;
  9. our D2 frame 1: the eye's wall sunlit, the rings' arc;
  10. looking up under Jupiter's deck: a bolt among the puffs.
- **How they were taken**:
  - the Mac build in Metal, full screen at 3024 × 1964;
  - a quality beyond Ultra, affordable on the Mac for stills (~10 fps): 64 samples, 2 light taps, full resolution,
    every pixel each frame (`t2q1i1o0p2l1r1n1`);
  - the dev scenes keep their own settings (`cloudpreset` does not reach them), so the quality is passed in the
    `cloudtest` / `clouddescent` value. Ultra itself is `cloudtest32t1q2i16o1p2l1r1n1`.
- **A photo mode, key P** (`DesktopInput.Photo.cs`, desktop mode only). It hides the interface (text, fps, altimeter,
  compass, shelf) and the Guides layer (orbits, the planets' circles), through `Renderer.forceRenderingOff` before
  each frame. TextMeshPro turns `enabled` back on by itself, hence that flag. At runtime "UI" sits under
  "Solar System", not at the scene's root. P again restores everything.
- **The scripts** (Mac only: the desktop build exists only there):
  - `Tools/mac_shots.sh <out> <seconds> "<desktopscript>" [app args]`: one scripted run of the app, full screen. It
    copies its PNGs (F12, one a second at most) and `Player.log` to `<out>`. The script's keys are the Input System's
    names; a wrong name throws and stops the script. Example: `"3:P,24:F12,25:+LeftArrow,26:-LeftArrow,28:F12,30:quit"`
    (the arrows turn at 90°/s).
  - `Tools/mac_cloud_gallery.sh [out]`: the whole gallery, 11 runs, ~12 min. It covers the `cloudtest` scenes 0-8
    (front, left, right, down, up, from 24 s once the volume is baked and the history settled), the night (one shot a
    second for the flashes), and the descents from 3,000 km at Jupiter (scene 3) and Saturn (scene 1) with `noorbits`.
    The ten pictures come from this script's own run (79 shots, no exceptions); only the choice was made by eye.
  - `Tools/contact_sheet.py <out>`: numbered sheets (`sheet_<run>.png`) to choose from; `--jpeg <png> <jpg> [width]`
    saves a choice.
  - The method is written in `docs/modules/Desktop.txt`, section SCREENSHOTS. It works for any view (`-goto`,
    `-autotravel`, `-desktoplook`, the eclipse presets). On Windows: the same method with a Standalone build and a
    PowerShell equivalent of `mac_shots.sh`, not made yet.
- **Not checked**: the EditMode tests were not rerun after the photo mode was added (the Mac build compiles).
