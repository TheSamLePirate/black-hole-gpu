# Carrier operations

## The carriers
The USS Gerald R. Ford (catapults, three arresting wires, an angled deck 9° to port), the Kiev and the Implacable (short take-offs, vertical landings). They steam into the wind at their speed: the deck moves, pitches and heaves in the swell.

## The F-35C on the wires
- The catapult: tensioned (full power), fired, 72 m/s over the deck at its end; the climb held at 8°.
- The approach: on speed — some 70 m/s at 19 t —, on the 3.5° glide path, lined up on the angled deck.
- The ball (the IFLOLS): the meatball against the green datum lights — above, high; below, low. Call the ball at ¾ NM.
- The wires: the hook catches the first it crosses; the target is the 2-wire. Full power at touchdown — a bolter's insurance.
- The LSO's calls: "power!", "you're low", "right for line-up", "wave off" (go around), "bolter" (missed the wires).
- The grades: _OK_ (perfect), OK, Fair, No grade, Cut (dangerous), B (bolter), WO (wave-off).

## The recoveries' cases
- Case I (day, clear): the initial 3 NM astern at 800 ft, 350 kt, the break past the bow, the downwind at 600 ft 1.2 NM abeam, the 180, the groove.
- Case II (clouds): the descent to ten miles at 800 ft, then the break.
- Case III (night, weather): the marshal 21 NM astern at 6 000 ft, the descent, dirty at ten miles, the platform at 1 200 ft until the glide path.

## The F-35B
- The vertical landing: up the port side, a hover alongside, across over the spot, down — some 2 t of fuel at most to hover.
- The short take-off: the nozzle aft, the rotation, the transition — some 150 m of deck at full mass.

## Common errors
Low in close (power at once), overcorrecting the line-up, rolling out of the 180 wide.
