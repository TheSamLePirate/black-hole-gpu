# Emergencies

## Loss of control
- ⇧X: back to stable flight — the wings rolled level, a gentle climb, a safe speed.
- In the direct law (no computer): unload (stick forward), power, roll wings level once flying. A spin: throttle idle, controls neutral, then against the rotation, then pull out of the dive.

## The ground
The Auto-GCAS pulls up at the last moment (5 g) — let it. The GPWS calls "terrain, pull up": pull and power, wings level.

## Engine and fuel
- The bingo: the fuel to reach the destination with its reserve — called by the flight plan; turn home at once.
- The fuel low: the nearest airfield (TARS: RTB), the cruise at Mach 0.8–0.85, high.
- The engine lost: glide at some 200 kt (best glide), the nearest field; the F-35 has one engine.

## The gear and the landing
- A hard landing: past 3 m/s down on a runway the gear is damaged (the F-35C's is built for the deck: 7.6 m/s).
- The missed approach: unstable at minimums — power, climb straight ahead, go round.

## In a fight
- A missile: the RWR's warning, beam it, flares, a break turn at the last moment.
- Hit: the damage shown, the jet can still fly — home.
- Ejection is not modelled: the craft destroyed, the flight restarts.
