# Formation flying

## The positions
- Echelon: on one side of the leader, 45° back, a wing-span or two apart.
- Fingertip / vic: the wingmen each side.
- Line abreast: beside each other, the tactical spread for mutual support.
- Trail: one behind the other.
The game holds the slots 15 m clear of both wingspans, 15 m back, for the close ones.

## The leader and the orders
The leader flies smoothly and calls: rejoin, tighten, loosen, attack, engage, cover, hold, home (RTB), break left/right. In the game: Tab held › Flight, or TARS's flight_lead tool. AI wingmen (up to three) fly real aerodynamics.

## Rejoining
From afar, an intercept onto the leader's track, then the slot approached from behind and below, the closure controlled (no overshoot): power back early, the speed matched at 50 m.

## Holding the slot
Small throttle inputs; the reference points on the leader's jet (the wingtip on the cockpit); in a turn on the outside, more power; on the inside, less.

## In the game
- On a room player's wing: Tab held › Flight › "Ana's wingman" (one entry per player flying; also "Formation" in the room's panel, or TARS: "join Ana"). Far from him (over 20 km), our jet is first put beside him; then the formation autopilot flies us to the first free place on his wing — his AI wingmen and the other players already on it counted, every page telling the room whose wing it flies; all taken, in trail behind him. The same entry again, or "Leave the formation": off, altitude, heading and speed held.
- Our own AI wingmen: Tab held › Flight › AI wingmen (each press one more, after three none), or TARS's flight_lead tool (0 to 3: Two, Three, Four).
- Flying on another's wing, the Auto-GCAS and Auto-ACAS are off; leading, the Auto-ACAS leaves alone our AI wingmen and the room's players flying on our wing: their close slots are flown on purpose.
