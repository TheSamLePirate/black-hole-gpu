# Formation flying

## The positions
- Echelon: on one side of the leader, 45° back, a wing-span or two apart.
- Fingertip / vic: the wingmen each side.
- Line abreast: beside each other, the tactical spread for mutual support.
- Trail: one behind the other.
The game holds the slots 15 m clear of both wingspans, 15 m back, for the close ones.

## The leader and the orders
The leader flies smoothly and calls: rejoin, tighten, loosen, attack, engage, cover, hold, home (RTB), break left/right. In the game: Tab held › Flight, or TARS's flight_lead tool. AI wingmen (up to three) fly real aerodynamics.

## Rejoining
From afar, an intercept onto the leader's track, then the slot approached from behind and below, the closure controlled (no overshoot): power back early, the speed matched at 50 m.

## Holding the slot
Small throttle inputs; the reference points on the leader's jet (the wingtip on the cockpit); in a turn on the outside, more power; on the inside, less.

## In the game
- Formation autopilot: "Formation" in the room's panel — our jet holds an echelon off another player's wing.
- The Auto-ACAS leaves the aircraft in formation with us alone.
