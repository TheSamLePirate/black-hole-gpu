# The ILS approach

## The system
The localizer gives the runway's centreline (its antenna beyond the far end; 2.5° full scale each side, a dot 1.25°), the glideslope the 3° path down to it (its antenna 300 m in; 0.7° full scale, a dot 0.35°). The needles: the course left or right of us, the glideslope above or below.

## The procedure in the game
1. The initial fix (IAF): 18.5 km out on the axis, at the glideslope's height or above; a hold on its racetrack if asked.
2. The localizer intercepted at 30–45°, led as the needle moves.
3. The glideslope met from below: the descent begun as the diamond centres — some 4 m/s down at 85 m/s. Already established inside the final fix (on the localizer, within a dot of the glideslope either way — a short final a little high or low), it is captured from there and the approach carried on; an established final is taken up the same way, not sent back to the initial fix.
4. The final fix (FAF): 9.3 km — gear and flaps, on speed, stable.
5. Minimums at 60 m: stable (within a dot) — continue; else the missed approach (climb straight ahead, back to the IAF).
6. The flare from 14 m, the throttle idle, touchdown in the zone 150–900 m in, braked to a stop.

## Flying it
- Small heading corrections, taken out early — never chase the needles.
- The descent rate: 5 × the ground speed in knots ft/min (some 4–4.5 m/s at 85 m/s).
- Power for the path, the nose for the speed on the final — the two together.

## In the game
"ILS approach" in the flight menu or the wheel, TARS's approach tool, or a flight plan's procedures (LNAV hands over at the IAF). The HUD shows the needles; the instructor calls the deviations and debriefs.

## The visual circuit
The flight school's circuit, flown by the approach's autopilot in a demonstration: the downwind 300 m over the field, 1.8 km abeam, 100 m/s, the gear down; from abeam the field, the base leg square to the runway at 45° of bank; the turn onto the final begun where a 40° bank turn at our speed rolls out on the centreline (the gap left as wide as that turn's own); down to the 3° path — 300 m over the field at most —, the gear and half flaps, the final's speed and a little; lined up: the final, the flare from 14 m, braked to a stop. Over the threshold still not lined up: go around.
