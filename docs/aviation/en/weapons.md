# The weapons

## The stores
- AIM-120C AMRAAM: radar-guided, beyond visual range — some 55 km, its own radar near the end.
- AIM-9X Sidewinder: a heat-seeker, close range — some 14 km —, off-boresight (the helmet's aim).
- GBU-32 JDAM: a 1 000 lb bomb guided by GPS on a ground target, released inside its zone.
- The 25 mm gun: some 220 rounds, inside 1 km; the HUD's funnel (the lead computed) on the target, a short burst.
- Flares and chaff (CMDS): to decoy a missile.
The loadouts: air-to-air (4 AIM-120, 2 AIM-9X), air-to-ground (2 AIM-120, 2 AIM-9X, 2 GBU-32), gun only.

## Shooting
- Lock the target (Tab), the weapon chosen (= / −), within its range (the HUD's launch zone: rMax, rMin), Enter to fire.
- The AIM-120: launch and leave once its radar is on; the AIM-9X: the tone, then fire.
- The gun: Space held; the funnel's wings on the target's span.

## The threats
- A SAM: its reach drawn in red on the map (some 32 km for an SA-6/SA-11 class); its missiles fired above 150 m over the ground.
- A missile warning: beam it, flares, a hard turn.

## Rules
Never fire on a neutral (an airliner) — identify first: an intercept to visual range.
