# The weapons

## The stores
- AIM-120C AMRAAM: radar-guided, beyond visual range — some 55 km, its own radar near the end.
- AIM-9X Sidewinder: a heat-seeker, close range — some 14 km —, off-boresight (the helmet's aim).
- GBU-32 JDAM: a 1 000 lb bomb guided by GPS on a ground target, released inside its zone.
- The 25 mm gun: some 220 rounds, inside 1 km; the HUD's funnel (the lead computed) on the target, a short burst.
- Flares and chaff (CMDS): to decoy a missile.
The loadouts: air-to-air (4 AIM-120, 2 AIM-9X), air-to-ground (2 AIM-120, 2 AIM-9X, 2 GBU-32), gun only.

## Shooting
- Lock the target (Tab), the weapon chosen (= / −), within its range (the HUD's launch zone: rMax, rMin), Enter to fire.
- The AIM-120: launch and leave once its radar is on; the AIM-9X: the tone, then fire.
- The gun: Space held; the funnel's wings on the target's span.

## The threats
- A SAM: its reach drawn in red on the map (some 32 km for an SA-6/SA-11 class); its missiles fired above 150 m over the ground.
- A missile warning: beam it, flares, a hard turn.

## The free fight in a room
In a room, in the F-35, outside a match, a mission, a lesson or a challenge (setting "Free fight in a room", on by default): the weapons live, the air-to-air stores, every craft a target — the other players, their AI wingmen and ours, the real airliners, ships and carriers the radar sees. An outside craft (an airliner, a ship, a carrier, another's wingman) is taken into the fight as a stand-in placed each frame where the radar has it — the one locked at any range, the others within 6 km; destroyed, the real one is gone for the whole room. Friendly fire is on; the radar is weapons free (a friend's track locks too). No time, no score to reach: shot down, in the air again 6 s later, 15 km south of where we fell, 5 000 m over the ground, heading north, tanks full. A match asked replaces it.

## Rules
Never fire on a neutral (an airliner) — identify first: an intercept to visual range. The free fight in a room alone makes every craft a target.
