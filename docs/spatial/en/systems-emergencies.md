# Systems and emergencies

## The consumables
Power: a battery fed by the generators (fuel cells, solar arrays, the Endurance's reactor) and drawn by the systems, the thrusters and the reaction wheels. Flat, the RCS and the wheels stop until it is charged back to 5 %. Oxygen: 0.84 kg per person per day; the CO₂ scrubbers have a life. The tablet's Systems page (M, then Systems), the cockpit's ECS screen and the crew's EECOM view show them.

## The failures
Drawn at the rate set (Settings › Spacecraft › Failures: none, rare, realistic, hard; never for a beginner). Each lights its alarm and the master caution, with WHY and WHAT TO DO.
- Main engine out: no thrust. Throttle to zero, check the feed pressure, wait 30 s, igniters armed, RELIGHT (one time in three only half the thrust after).
- RCS thruster stuck open: the craft drifts by itself. Identify the quad by the drift, hold the attitude with the SAS, close its isolation valves, ISOLATE THE QUAD (a quarter of the RCS's authority lost).
- IMU drift: the held attitude slides. Compare with the star tracker, release the holds, select the backup IMU.
- Main bus short: three times the systems' draw. Shed the loads, open the faulty bus's breaker, tie the essential loads to bus B.
- Cabin oxygen leak: the stock empties in about six hours. Suits on, find the leak by the pressure drop, close the tank's cross-feed valve.
- CO₂ scrubber failed: check the partial pressure, fit the spare cartridge's adapter, restart the fan.

## The checklists
A failure's action is given only once its checklist is all ticked: on the tablet, on the cockpit's CKL screen, or from a crew console (the ticks are shared). The normal checklists (before launch, orbit insertion, rendezvous and docking, deorbit and entry, powered landing) are read the same way.

## Protection
The auto-protection (Settings › Spacecraft) hovers a craft falling toward the ground and stops one closing on a station too fast — a caution first, then it takes over.
